Ticket delivery policy

Every Ganor ticket is digital. It arrives the moment you RSVP or pay, and nothing is ever shipped.

Last updated 21 November 2026

{{icon:ticket}}Digital tickets only

Ganor sells digital tickets and takes free RSVPs for events. We don’t sell or ship physical goods, and there’s no delivery fee.

{{icon:clock}}When you get your ticket

  • Straight away: as soon as you RSVP, or your payment goes through, your ticket opens on screen with its QR code.
  • A private link: your ticket also has its own private link, so you can open it again on any phone.
  • By email: if you give an email address, we send the ticket there too.
  • On WhatsApp: one tap sends the ticket link to your own WhatsApp, so it’s easy to find on the day.

{{icon:qr-code}}What your ticket shows

Your name, the event, its date, time and venue, your ticket type, a QR code and a ticket code. Each ticket works once: the first scan at the gate is the one that counts, so keep your QR code and link to yourself.

{{icon:scan}}At the event

Show your ticket on your phone at the gate. The host’s team scans the QR code, or types the ticket code, to check you in. The host and venue decide who is let in and the rules on the day.

{{icon:warning}}If your ticket doesn’t arrive

  • Check your email’s spam or promotions folder, and look for the ticket link in your WhatsApp.
  • Ask the host: they can resend your ticket from their dashboard using your phone number.
  • If you paid but no ticket appeared, or you can’t reach the host, message us with the phone number or email you used. We’ll find your ticket and send it again.

If you were charged but no ticket was issued, see the refund policy.

{{icon:calendar}}If the event changes or is cancelled

Your ticket stays valid for the new date or venue. If the host cancels a paid event, you get your money back as set out in the refund policy.
