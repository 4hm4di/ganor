{{icon:compass}}Getting started

Ganor is where you find what’s happening near you. Hosts create an event, share one link, take free RSVPs or sell tickets, and scan guests in at the gate. Guests open the link, get their ticket with a QR code, and show it at the door.

Ganor opens soon. These docs describe how it works when it opens. Join the list and we’ll message you on WhatsApp the day you can start.

Who Ganor is for

  • Hosts: anyone running a gathering, from conferences, summits, trade fairs and cooperative launches to lectures, campus events and shows.
  • Gate teams: the people a host trusts to scan guests in.
  • Guests: anyone looking for something to go to, or holding a ticket.
  • Businesses: shops, hospitals, restaurants, hotels and more, listed on Ganor Places.

Hosts and gate teams work in the Ganor app at app.ganor.ng. Guests don’t need an account.

{{icon:user}}Your host account

Signing up

Create your host account at app.ganor.ng with your email and a password, then open the link we email you to confirm your address. Or tap Continue with Google to sign up and sign in with your Google account.

Your host profile

Your name, logo and a few lines about you appear on your event pages. Add how guests can reach you: a WhatsApp or call number is required before you can publish. Email, website and Instagram are optional.

Staying safe

  • One device at a time. Signing in on a new phone or computer signs your account out everywhere else.
  • Automatic sign-out. After 30 minutes without activity, or when you close the browser, you’re signed out.
  • Your bank account for payouts is added once in your dashboard and checked before money is paid out.

{{icon:calendar}}Create an event

Tap New event and choose what kind it is:

  • Free RSVP: guests register for free. Choose a plan by how many guests you expect (see Free RSVP events).
  • Paid tickets: guests buy tickets (see Paid tickets).
  1. Give your event a name, a category, a date and start and end times.
  2. Add the city, the venue and its address so guests can find you.
  3. Add a banner for the top of your event page and a few lines about what to expect.
  4. If it applies, tick Halal-friendly (see Halal-friendly events).
  5. Save. Your event stays a draft, visible only to you, until you publish it.

Before you can publish, Ganor checks that your profile has a WhatsApp or call number, that a paid event has at least one ticket type, and that a paid RSVP plan has been paid. You can change the details after you publish; tell your guests about any change to the date, time or venue.

{{icon:users}}Free RSVP events

A Free RSVP event costs your guests nothing. Each guest gives their name and phone number, answers any questions you add, and gets a ticket with a QR code to show at the gate. One phone number can RSVP once per event.

Your questions

Add questions to the RSVP form, such as organisation, job title or t-shirt size, and mark which ones are required. You can download your guest list with their answers.

{{icon:ticket}}Paid tickets

A paid event can have as many ticket types as you need, for example Regular, VIP and Table for 10. For each one you set:

  • The price. Every paid ticket costs at least ₦1,000. Paid events can’t have free tickets; for a free event, create a Free RSVP event.
  • How many are available, and the most one person can buy in an order.
  • What’s included, such as front rows, lunch or a certificate (optional).

You can pause or reopen any ticket type at any time. Once a ticket type has sold, its price is locked; add a new ticket type for a different price.

Online payments are switching on soon. You can set up and publish paid events now; the event page shows that ticket sales open soon until card, transfer and USSD payments go live.

{{icon:share}}Share your event

Every event gets one link, like app.ganor.ng/e/your-event. Post it on WhatsApp, your status, Instagram or anywhere else. The link shows a clean preview with your banner, date and venue, and your event page has buttons to share it on WhatsApp, X, Facebook and LinkedIn.

Guests who open the link can RSVP or buy straight away. They don’t need an account or an app.

{{icon:percent}}Fees and payouts

Free events on the Free plan cost nothing. On paid tickets, the Ganor fee is 6% of the ticket price + ₦100 per ticket. Card and bank transfer charges are included in that fee. There is no setup fee and no monthly fee. Larger free events use a plan instead.

Who pays the fee

You choose, per event:

  • Buyer pays the fee: the fee is added at checkout. You receive your full ticket price.
  • You absorb the fee: buyers pay your ticket price only, and the fee comes out of each sale.

Payouts

Add the bank account for your payouts in your dashboard. Ganor checks it before any money is paid out. Your dashboard shows your sales, your take-home and your payout details, so you always know where your money is.

{{icon:scan}}Gate check-in

Any phone becomes the scanner. Open your event and tap Gate check-in, or let your gate team scan for you. Point the camera at a guest’s QR code, or type the code under it. You’ll see one of these results:

  • Admitted: the ticket is valid. Let the guest in.
  • Already checked in: this ticket was scanned before. The screen shows when.
  • Not a valid ticket: the code isn’t for this event. Send the guest to your help desk.
  • Not assigned: this gate account isn’t set up for this event. Ask the host to tick it.

Each ticket works once, so the same QR code can’t get two people in. The scanner shows how many guests are in so far.

Your gate team

  1. In your dashboard, open Gate team and add each person with their own username and password.
  2. Tick the events each person can check in. They see only those events.
  3. Share their sign-in details with Copy or Send on WhatsApp. They sign in at app.ganor.ng/gate.
  • A password is shown once, when you set it. If someone loses theirs, set a new one; it signs them out on their phone.
  • Each gate account works on one phone at a time. Signing in on another phone signs out the first.
  • You can switch an account off or delete it at any time.

{{icon:user}}For guests

Getting your ticket

Open the event link. For a free event, enter your name, your phone number and any details the host asks for, then tap RSVP. For a paid event, choose your tickets and pay by card, bank transfer or USSD once sales are open.

Your ticket appears straight away with its QR code. Keep it on your phone (a screenshot works) and show it at the gate. Each ticket gets one person in, once.

Questions about an event

Contact the host. The host runs the event, so questions about the venue, the programme, changes or refunds go to them. Every event page has the host’s WhatsApp or phone number.

When to contact Ganor

Message us if you paid but your ticket didn’t appear, if you were charged twice, or if you can’t reach the host about a cancelled event. See the refund policy.

{{icon:halal}}Halal-friendly events

Hosts can mark an event Halal-friendly so guests who care about it can find it with the Halal-friendly filter. Say in your event details what applies. The label can mean:

How the event is run

  • Halal food and drinks only, and no alcohol.
  • Prayer times kept in the programme, with a place to pray.
  • A modest dress code, or separate seating for men and women.

What the event is about

  • Islamic finance and non-interest banking.
  • Halal investment, savings, takaful and zakat.
  • Halal products, trade and certification, such as halal expos and Muslim business fairs.
  • Islamic lectures, Eid fests and community gatherings.
This is the host’s description. Ganor doesn’t certify events as halal. If an event isn’t what the host said, tell us and we’ll look into it. Using the label falsely counts as a misleading event under our terms.

{{icon:shop}}For businesses

Every listing is checked in person, with directions and a working phone number. Ganor Places comes after events. To be among the first listed, join the list and choose A local business.
