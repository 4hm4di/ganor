{{icon:info}}Who we are

Ganor (“we”) runs ganor.ng and the Ganor app at app.ganor.ng, and is responsible for the personal data described here. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

{{icon:doc}}What we collect

When you join the list

Your name, your WhatsApp number or email (you choose), whether you’re a host, a guest or a business, and the kind of event you’re interested in, if you tell us.

When you use Ganor

  • Host account: your name and email, and your password (stored scrambled, so no one can read it). If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us.
  • Host profile: your logo, a few lines about you, and your contact details: WhatsApp or call number, and email, website and Instagram if you add them. These are shown on your event pages.
  • Guests: the name and phone number on each RSVP or ticket, your email if the host asks for it, your answers to the host’s questions, what you got and when it was scanned at the gate. Each RSVP or ticket also gets a private ticket link.
  • Stopping fake RSVPs: when you RSVP we keep a scrambled code for your browser and for your network, so we can limit how many RSVPs come from one phone or one place. We never store your IP address or a readable device ID.
  • Gate team: each gate account’s name and username, which events it can check in, when it signed in, and the tickets it scanned.
  • Ratings: if you agreed at checkout, the star rating and comment you leave after an event, the name you choose to show, and whether your ticket was scanned.
  • Hosts and businesses: event or business details, and the bank account for payouts.
  • Payments: handled by our licensed payment partners. We receive confirmation of payment, not your card details.

Cookies

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. The Ganor app uses only the cookies it needs to work: keeping you signed in (they end when you close your browser), signing you out after 30 minutes without activity, keeping a gate phone signed in, and showing a short message after you save something, and one cookie that lets us limit how many RSVPs one phone can make for an event. The ganor.ng website stores one small note in your browser so the opening animation only plays once per visit.

{{icon:settings}}How we use it

  • To run your account, keep it to one device at a time, and keep it secure.
  • To deliver tickets and check guests in at the gate.
  • To process payments and pay hosts.
  • To send you one WhatsApp message after an event asking you to rate it, only if you ticked that box at checkout. Reply STOP and we won’t send another.
  • To show guests’ ratings of events and hosts, so people can choose events with confidence.
  • To tell you when Ganor opens, if you joined the list.
  • To keep Ganor safe and stop fraud, such as fake RSVPs.
  • To meet our legal duties.

We never sell your personal data.

{{icon:users}}Who we share it with

  • The host of an event you attend: your name, phone number, email if asked, answers to their questions, your ticket type and your check-in, so they can run their event. Hosts may only use this for that event.
  • The host’s gate team: what they need to check you in, such as your name and ticket type when your code is scanned.
  • Everyone who visits Ganor: a host’s profile and contact details on their event pages. If you leave a rating, your stars, your comment and either your first name and last initial or “Verified attendee” (your choice). Never a guest’s phone number. The host can reply publicly.
  • Payment partners: to process payments and payouts.
  • Service providers: to run Ganor, send emails and WhatsApp messages, and deliver website form submissions to our inbox. Some are outside Nigeria, including Supabase (UK), Vercel, Brevo (email), Google (if you sign in with Google) and Meta (WhatsApp). We use their data protection terms to keep your data safe.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

{{icon:clock}}How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as we need it: account data while your account is open, ticket and payment records for as long as the law requires, gate sign-in records for up to a week after they end, and waitlist details until Ganor opens or until you ask us to delete them. Rating links and the records of who agreed to rating messages are deleted 6 months after ratings for that event close. Published reviews stay up until you ask us to remove them; they are linked to a coded reference, not your phone number.

{{icon:shield}}Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it or delete it.
  • Stop using it for messages about Ganor, including rating requests (or just reply STOP).
  • Remove a review you left.
  • Give you a copy to take elsewhere.

Message us and we’ll reply within 30 days. If you’re unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

{{icon:lock}}Security

We protect your data with access controls, encryption in transit and scrambled passwords. Accounts work on one device at a time and sign out when idle. If a breach puts your data at risk, we’ll tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires.
