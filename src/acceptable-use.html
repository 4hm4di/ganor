Acceptable use policy

Ganor is for real events, run honestly. This page sets out what’s allowed, what isn’t, and what we do about it.

{{icon:info}}Who this applies to

This policy applies to everyone who uses Ganor: hosts who run events, gate team members who scan tickets, and guests who RSVP or buy tickets. It is part of our terms of use.

{{icon:check-circle}}What Ganor is for

Ganor is for listing real events, taking free RSVPs, selling tickets to them and checking guests in. For example:

  • Conferences, summits, trade fairs, exhibitions and workshops.
  • Concerts, comedy shows, film screenings and cultural events.
  • Religious, community and charity gatherings.
  • Weddings, naming ceremonies and other private events.
  • Sports, fitness and outdoor events.

A ticket on Ganor is entry to an event. It can include what the host says comes with it, such as a meal or an event pack, but it can’t be used to sell something else.

{{icon:close-circle}}What isn’t allowed

You can’t use Ganor to list, promote or sell tickets for any of these:

  • Anything illegal in Nigeria, or an event without the permits, licences or venue permission the law requires.
  • Weapons, ammunition or explosives, or events that sell or hand them out.
  • Illegal drugs or controlled substances, and alcohol or tobacco for anyone under 18.
  • Adult, sexual or escort content or services.
  • Gambling, betting, lotteries or raffles, unless the host holds the licence the law requires.
  • Investment, trading or “money-making” schemes that promise returns, including forex or crypto signals, Ponzi, pyramid or multi-level recruitment schemes.
  • Goods, services, memberships, loans or donations dressed up as tickets.
  • Counterfeit or stolen goods, or events that copy another brand or event to mislead people.
  • Hate, violence, harassment or discrimination, or groups that promote them.
  • Fake events, events you don’t run or don’t have the right to sell tickets for, or details meant to mislead guests.

{{icon:user}}How everyone must use Ganor

  • Give true details about yourself, your event and your payout account. Don’t pretend to be someone else.
  • Take ticket payments for Ganor events only through Ganor’s checkout, and don’t use it to move money for anything other than tickets.
  • Don’t make fake purchases, test stolen cards or try to get money back unfairly.
  • Hosts use guests’ details only to run their event, and don’t send them spam.
  • Guests don’t copy tickets or resell them for more than they paid.
  • Gate accounts are only for checking guests in at the host’s events.
  • Don’t try to break, overload, scrape or get around the security of Ganor.

{{icon:shield}}How we check

  • Hosts confirm their email address, and must add a WhatsApp or call number guests can reach before they can publish.
  • A host’s paid tickets only go on sale once Ganor has confirmed that the name on their payout bank account matches their bank’s record.
  • We may review any event, and we look into every report we receive from guests or anyone else.
  • Our payment partner also runs its own checks on payments.

{{icon:warning}}If the rules are broken

Depending on what happened, we may:

  • Ask the host to change the event, or take the event down.
  • Pause ticket sales while we look into a problem.
  • Refund guests, for example when an event was fake or misleading.
  • Switch off gate accounts, or suspend or close the account.
  • Report it to our payment partner, and to the authorities where the law requires.
